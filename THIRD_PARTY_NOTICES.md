## Everything else

All sprites, UI art and the town map are original, generated by the scripts in `tools/` (see
[docs/ASSETS.md](docs/ASSETS.md)). The seed data (familiars, vendors, ratings, Boons, Banes, townsfolk and their Quest
Logs) is fictional sample data written for AgentScape. It describes no real product.
